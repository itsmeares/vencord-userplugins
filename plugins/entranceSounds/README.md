# EntranceSounds

EntranceSounds unlocks Discord's native Entrance Sound controls without requiring Nitro. It supports an account-wide sound, per-server overrides, and cross-server Soundboard sounds.

[Install the plugins first](../../README.md#install-on-windows), then enable `EntranceSounds` in Discord under `Vencord` > `Plugins`.

## Use it

1. Open Discord settings, then go to `Voice & Video` > `Sounds` > `Entrance Sounds`.
2. Choose a sound for all servers or select one server and give it a different sound.
3. Join a voice channel.

The selected sound plays through your outgoing voice connection. External Soundboard sounds that Discord normally limits to Nitro use the same path.

Right-click a voice channel if you want to join once without playing your entrance sound.

## What other people hear

Other users hear the entrance sound as voice audio. Discord's server does not emit the native Soundboard animation or attribution for non-Nitro cross-server playback.

Your Soundboard volume controls how loudly the mixed sound is sent. A listener can change your user volume, but that changes your microphone and the sound together.

Playback stops when the sound finishes, when you leave voice, or when you deafen yourself. Discord still handles screen sharing through its native media engine.

Camera transport is enabled, but it has not yet been tested with a physical webcam.
